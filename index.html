About this shop

Intertwingularity is Ted Nelson's word for the idea that nothing can be understood on its own: everything is tangled up with everything else. An online shop pretends the opposite. Every item has its own price, its own box, its own button. So we built a shop and let the tangle back in. Our sketches are the products. Add one and its neighbors come with it. Prices move when you touch them. Buy light and the room gets darker. Nothing here is for sale alone.
